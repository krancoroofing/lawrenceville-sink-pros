Why Kitchen Sinks in Lawrenceville Homes Clog the Way They Do

A kitchen sink rarely blocks all at once. It starts with a slow drain after washing up, then a gurgle when the dishwasher empties, then standing water that takes an hour to clear. In Lawrenceville homes the pattern behind it is familiar: cooking grease rinsed down with hot water cools inside the line and coats the pipe walls, coffee grounds and rice swell and settle at the trap, and the narrowed pipe catches a little more debris every week until nothing passes at all.

Lawrenceville Sink Pros works on exactly this problem — kitchen sink unclogging Lawrenceville GA households call when a plunger and a bottle of cleaner have stopped making any difference. The first step is always finding where the blockage actually sits, because grease built up along a long horizontal run needs a different clearing method than solids packed into the P-trap under the sink.

Our Kitchen Drain Services

Kitchen Sink Unclogging

The main service: locating the blockage, clearing it fully, and then running a full sink of water through the line to confirm the drain is genuinely open — not just draining around a partial clog that will close again within days.

Drain Cleaning Services

When buildup lines the whole pipe rather than blocking one spot, the line itself needs cleaning. Scouring the grease and residue off the pipe walls restores the full width of the drain so clogs stop reforming in the same place.

Emergency Drain Unclogging

A sink that backs up during cooking, with guests in the house or a dishwasher full of dirty water, cannot wait for a convenient slot. Emergency unclogging gets the line open so the kitchen is usable again.

Sink Drain Snaking

A drain snake reaches blockages past the trap that no surface method can touch. The auger breaks through compacted grease, food waste and other obstructions and pulls back what it loosens.

Hydro Jetting for Kitchen Sinks

For lines narrowed by years of grease, high-pressure water jetting strips the buildup from the pipe walls along the full run. It suits older Lawrenceville homes where the same drain has clogged repeatedly.

Kitchen Sink Repair and Installation

Leaking traps, worn fittings and damaged sink connections are repaired, and new kitchen sinks are installed with the drain assembly connected and tested before the job is finished.

Sewer Line Cleaning

When several drains in the house slow down together, the problem may sit in the main sewer line rather than the kitchen branch. Sewer line cleaning clears the shared line so every fixture drains properly.

Signs Your Kitchen Sink Needs Professional Unclogging

  • Water pools in the sink and drains away slowly
  • Gurgling noises from the drain when the dishwasher runs
  • A rotten-egg or sour smell rising from the sink
  • Water backing up when the garbage disposal is switched on
  • Both sides of a double sink filling at the same time
  • The same clog returning a week or two after home remedies

A clog that keeps returning in the same sink is almost never bad luck — it means buildup inside the line is catching new debris. Clearing the line itself, not just the blockage, is what ends the cycle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my kitchen sink smell like rotten eggs?

That smell usually comes from food waste and grease decomposing inside the trap or a partially blocked line, where water sits instead of flowing through. Clearing the blockage and cleaning the trap removes the source of the odor.

Is hydro jetting better than snaking?

They solve different problems. Snaking punches through a blockage so water flows again; hydro jetting also scrubs the pipe walls clean, which matters when grease buildup along the whole line is why the clog formed. The right choice depends on where the problem sits.
